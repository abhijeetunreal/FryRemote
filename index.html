Engineered for Precision

⚡
Ultra-Low Latency
Asynchronous event loop with tokio sends key events in sub-millisecond times directly to Google TV port 6466.
🔒
Encrypted Pairing
Full implementation of Google's TLS pairing protocol with client RSA certificates and pin challenge verification.
🔍
Zero-Config Discovery
Automatic multicast DNS discovery detects all Android TV and Google TV devices on your local Wi-Fi.
🚀
Quick App Launchers
Direct deep-linking shortcuts for YouTube, Netflix, Prime Video, Disney+, Hotstar, and Spotify.

FryRemote is an open-source project written with Rust and egui.
